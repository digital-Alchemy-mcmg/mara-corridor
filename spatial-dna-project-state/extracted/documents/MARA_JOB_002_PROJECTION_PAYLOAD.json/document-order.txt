Evaluation Principle: Require verifiable active status or recent execution timestamps to satisfy external compliance gates.
Historical Trajectory Evidence (decay_rule: none):
Early-career underwriting or foundational supervisory roles.
Evaluation Principle: Explains developmental momentum and baseline discipline, but is not required to establish present capability.
