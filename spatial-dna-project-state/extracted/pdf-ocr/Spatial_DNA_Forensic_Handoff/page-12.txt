Provenance and Authority Tiers

PROVEN BY ARTIFACT: Physical, inspectable machine
code, JSON, or workbooks committed/staged.

PROVEN BY EXECUTION: Operations run,
mathematically verified, producing
producing reproducible output.

REPORTED / EXPERIMENTAL: Interactive
prototyping observed but not chained in CI/CD.

CLAIMED BUT NOT VERIFIED: Exists in design
specs — but not executed autonomously.

SOURCE-REPORTED: Concepts referenced in
docs without underlying raw exported artifacts.
